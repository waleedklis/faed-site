A quick example: from a bank SMS to a number you understand

This is the whole idea of the app in three steps. Everything you see here happens inside your phone, with no internet and no link to your bank.

The big number is not your balance. It is what you can spend today if the money is to last to the end of the month: what is left of your budget, divided by the days remaining.

More examples of what the app does

Example: the message that is not an expense

SNB-AlAhli
Outgoing transfer
Amount: SAR 500.00
To: your other account ****902
On: 15/10/26

This is a transfer between your own accounts, so Faed files it under “Between my accounts” and does not count it as spending — otherwise your money would look as though it had left you twice. In the same way, money someone hands you to pay on their behalf goes to “Held for someone (not mine)”, and a store refund is deducted from your spending rather than counted as income.

1. Getting started in three steps

  1. Language and name. Pick Arabic or English and, if you like, your first name for the greeting.
  2. Your approximate salary. A rough number is enough. From it the app works out how much you can spend per day, starts your financial month on payday automatically, and recognises your salary bank from your messages. No fixed salary? Tap Skip.
  3. Bank messages. Read and accept the disclosure, allow SMS access, then pick the banks and wallets you want read. Tap Start.
During the first week the app shows numbers only, with no insights. Insights appear gradually as it learns your pattern.

Installed from a file outside the Play Store?

Android may refuse the SMS permission. Open App info on your phone → the three-dot menu → Allow restricted settings, then request the permission again.

2. Banks and wallets

Faed reads messages from licensed banks and wallets only: Al Rajhi, SNB (AlAhli), Alinma, Riyad, SAB, Albilad, Aljazira, BSF, ANB, SAIB, Vision Bank, D360, STC Bank, stc pay, urpay, Barq, Tiqmo, meem, Mobily Pay and others.

  • Promotional senders, such as names ending in “AD” or “Offers”, are never read, even when they belong to a bank.
  • Shops, telecom operators, government services and insurance companies are not read. They appear greyed out in Settings under “Other senders”.
  • OTP codes, welcome messages, transaction-limit notices and declined-transaction alerts are never counted as transactions.

My bank is missing

Send us the sender name exactly as it appears in your Messages app via Settings → Send feedback, or share masked samples from Advanced settings → Help us support your bank. Numbers and names are masked before sending and you review the text yourself.

3. Understanding the home screen

  • Spent: everything that left your accounts this month, excluding transfers between your own accounts, savings, and ATM withdrawals when cash tracking is on.
  • Income: money that came in from outside your accounts. A cash deposit or a transfer from your other account is not income.
  • Budget left / Salary left: the label tells you what the remaining amount is based on. If you set a budget it is the remainder of that; otherwise of your salary; otherwise of this month's income.
  • You can spend … per day: the remaining amount divided by the days left until the end of your financial month.
  • Explain this number: a button under each card that breaks any figure down into its transactions.
Over budget but still within your salary? The explanation under the card mentions both.

4. Categories

Transactions are categorised automatically from the merchant name and the message text. Open any transaction to change its category; the app asks whether to apply it to this transaction only or to every transaction from that merchant. Choosing “All” creates a rule that also applies to future transactions.

Special categories

  • Between my accounts: a transfer from your account to your other account. Counted as neither spending nor income. Detected automatically, even when the bank adds a fee, once you enter the variants of your name under Settings → Your names in transfers.
  • Held for someone (not mine): money that passed through your account but was not yours, such as an amount you received for a friend and forwarded. Counted as neither income nor spending; only the transfer fee is counted.
  • Refunds: money returned for something you paid. Open the incoming transaction and choose “Refund of an expense”; it is deducted from that category's spending instead of being counted as income.
  • Savings and gold: a transfer to a savings account or a gold purchase. Not spending.

Renaming a merchant

Open the transaction → Edit details → change the name. “In all transactions” renames it in past and future transactions and keeps its category.

5. Cash and ATM

Turn on “Cash on hand” from the home screen and enter the amount you currently have. After that:

  • ATM withdrawals are added to cash and not counted as spending, so they are not counted twice.
  • What you spend in cash you record with the “Spent cash” button; that is the real expense.
  • Cash deposits are deducted from cash only when you have enough cash to cover them. Otherwise they appear struck through; tap one if it really came from your cash.
  • If you recategorise an ATM withdrawal, for example as “Personal care”, it becomes a direct expense and does not enter cash.

6. Balance

The app does not connect to your bank. Each account balance is computed as: the last known balance, either stated by the bank in a message or entered by you, plus every income after it, minus every expense and its fees.

Why does it differ from the bank app?

  • A held amount you have not received a message about yet, or a released hold that returned to the account without a message.
  • A foreign-currency purchase that was estimated. Enter the amount the bank actually charged in the transaction details.
  • An old balance you entered a while ago. Tap “Your balance now” → the bank → enter the balance as shown in the bank app.
A negative balance on a current account means money came in without a message. Update the balance from the bank app and the calculation resumes from the new figure.

7. Foreign-currency purchases

Some banks report international purchases in the foreign currency only. The app converts US dollars, Gulf currencies and the Jordanian dinar to riyals at their fixed rates and adds the estimated international-purchase fee. The amount is shown with ≈ and the original amount in the details. Enter the actual amount from your statement to replace the estimate.

Currencies with floating rates, such as the euro and the pound, stay in their own currency until you enter the riyal amount.

8. Simple mode

From Settings → Simple mode. The home screen shows how much you spent, how much you can spend per day, income and remaining, your balance and cash, the top three categories and the last three transactions. The bottom bar has Home, the add button and Settings only. “Show full details” brings everything back.

New users start in simple mode automatically.

9. Backup and moving between devices

Your data lives on your phone only. From Settings → Backup → Save a copy you get a file containing transactions, categories, balances and all settings. On the new device choose Restore your backup and skip setup on the first screen.

The Play Store edition and the direct-download edition are separate apps, each with its own data. Move between them with a backup and do not use both at once.

10. Privacy in brief

  • The app has no internet permission. Nothing leaves your phone.
  • It reads messages from the senders you chose only, and ignores OTP codes.
  • You can revoke the SMS permission or delete all data at any time from Settings.
  • Feedback and samples are sent from your own email, after you review the text and numbers and names are masked.

Full text in the privacy policy.

11. FAQ

A transaction appeared that is not real

Open it and tap “Not a real transaction? Delete it”. If a bank message was misread, tap “Misread? Report it” so we receive the format and fix it.

My spending is high but I did not spend that much

Look in “What makes up your spending” for a transfer to your other account or a pass-through amount, and categorise it as “Between my accounts” or “Held for someone”.

A transaction dated tomorrow

Some banks write a wrong date in evening messages. The app corrects it automatically from the message arrival time.

The screen stutters while scrolling

From Settings → Glass look choose “Off”. “Auto” turns it off by itself on mid-range devices.

How do I send feedback?

From the home screen or Settings → Send feedback. Your email app opens with a ready message to support@faed.app. Or post your question in the forum.
